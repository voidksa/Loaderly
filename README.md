Record your screen, browse and download media, edit video and audio, add captions, and make visualizers. Loaderly brings these tools into one desktop workspace for Windows, macOS, and Linux, with English, Arabic, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese interfaces.

Windows 11 is recommended for the best experience. Loaderly may run on Windows 10, but correct operation of every feature is not guaranteed.

Windows installation and updates are managed by Microsoft Store. The Mac and Linux packages for 2.6.0 have been refreshed to receive future in-app updates. If Loaderly shows the update notice, download the current package and install it once over your existing copy, even if you already downloaded 2.6.0. Keep your recordings and settings folders.

Mac requires macOS 13.3 or later. Quit Loaderly, open the DMG, and replace the app in Applications. Mac builds are not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. See the [installation guide](https://loaderly.app/docs/installation/) for additional requirements.

## New in 2.6.3

- Faster supported direct downloads. Direct files can download through several connections when the source supports it. Your speed limit is respected, and incompatible sources use the regular download method.
- Your messages, within reach. Open My messages from the top bar and keep using Loaderly while the conversation is open. Talk to the team and Refresh sit together above your requests.
- A steadier conversation. New replies arrive without rebuilding the conversation or interrupting your draft. Message fields have one clear focus outline and follow your theme.
- Clearer download settings. Download settings are easier to find. Download settings and several editing dialogs now open in separate windows with the app theme and platform controls.
- Check updates from Settings. The update check in Settings now has access to the same update service used by the main menu.

## New in 2.6.2

- A clear saved rating. After sending, your rating and any suggestion stay read-only for this release. Empty suggestion fields are hidden, and the stars no longer show unwanted circles.
- An organized account menu. Your identity and account actions have clearer spacing and alignment in every supported language.
- Conversations in your account. Contact the Loaderly team and follow replies in My requests, in the app or on the website with the same account. Keep one active request at a time and share images or videos by Gofile or Catbox link.
- Clearer team messages. Messages from Loaderly can include formatted text and a button with a clear label in your language.
- A support window that fits your workspace. My requests follows your theme, corner style and the glass effects supported by your system. Menus, buttons and text alignment are consistent across all supported languages.
- Recording settings with more room. Audio sources, camera settings and shortcuts have clearer spacing in the recording sidebar, including right-to-left layouts.
- Keep the browser sidebar visible. Corrects a layout issue that could cover the media sidebar on some Windows systems. The correction applies to all supported interface languages.

## New in 2.6.1

- Send your rating with confidence. The send button stays visible. If you need to sign in or sending is temporarily unavailable, Loaderly explains the next step and keeps your draft for another try.
- Contact us without leaving Loaderly. Open Contact & feedback from your account or the menu. Send help requests, problems and ideas using your signed-in email, and get a request reference when your message is accepted. Rating is also one click away in your account.
- Check for updates from Settings. Check your installed version, look for a new release and follow its download or Microsoft Store status without leaving Settings.

## New in 2.6.0

- A redesigned workspace for Media, recording, editing, and My designs, with settings search and a collapsible sidebar.
- A built-in browser with tabs, bookmarks, page privacy tools, and detected-media downloads. Preview quality options and choose items from long playlists.
- A new default Loaderly cursor and an expanded pack catalog. Preview all cursor states and motion, with softer tilt settling, continuous motion blur, animated waiting indicators, and click effects that finish during a long press.
- A resizable timeline, direct preview positioning, flexible zoom endings, 24 new backgrounds, and individual silence-cut review.
- On-device transcription, translation, and dubbing in 23 languages. Reuse reviewed captions and adjust each speaker's voice and timing.
- My designs stores themes, visualizers, microphone filters, sound presets, and editing layouts locally. Share an exported file without an account. The online Community catalog has retired.
- A new recording studio and floating controls across Windows, macOS, and Linux. Capture and audio options depend on the operating system and devices.

Read the [2.6.0 release notes](https://github.com/voidksa/Loaderly/releases/tag/v2.6.0).

## Browser privacy

[Loaderly Web Privacy](https://loaderly.app/browser-privacy/) also lets you blur or hide selected webpage elements in your browser before recording. It follows elements as you scroll, works with any screen recorder, and does not require pairing or an account.
