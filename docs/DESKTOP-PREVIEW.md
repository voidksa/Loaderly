# Install Loaderly 2.1

Download Loaderly for your computer from [the release page](https://github.com/voidksa/Loaderly/releases/tag/v2.1.0).

## Windows

Open **Loaderly-Setup-2.1.0.exe**, choose where to install the app and follow the installer. Finish any active downloads or exports before updating. Your library and preferences are preserved.

## macOS

1. In **Apple menu → About This Mac**, check whether your Mac has an Apple chip or an Intel processor.
2. Download **mac-arm64.zip** for Apple Silicon or **mac-x64.zip** for Intel. macOS 12 or newer is required.
3. Quit an older Loaderly with **Command+Q**, open the ZIP and move **Loaderly.app** into **Applications**.
4. Open Loaderly from Applications. If macOS blocks it, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** for Loaderly. Keep your system security enabled.

## Linux

Use the **amd64.deb** on Ubuntu or Debian and open it with your software installer. Alternatively, download the **x86_64.AppImage**, allow it to run as a program in its file properties, and open it. AppImage needs FUSE support from your distribution. Run Loaderly as your normal user.

## Your first clip

Choose **Library → Import** to open a local video or audio file. Select it, choose **Open editor**, make an adjustment and export a new file. Your original stays in place.

Open **Community library** to find themes, visualizers and sound presets. **Create & share** lets you publish a design directly from Loaderly. Themes work across Windows, macOS and Linux; desktop glass adapts to system support.
